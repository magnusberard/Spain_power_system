How the numbers are made

  • Model: upward energy of phase I per hour (rd_phase1_summary.csv, up_mwh), 53 weekly chunks run in order. Phase II's down side and the real-time AC stage are not included.
  • REE: e·sios hourly series of technical constraints, phase I, upward, for 2024 (as in the results viewer's reference data).
  • Correlation: Pearson, as for prices on the results viewer, on hourly values and on daily, weekly and monthly sums. Average day: the 24 hourly means over the year.
  • Monthly error: the sum of |model − REE| over the twelve months, as a share of REE's yearly volume.
  • Partial trading gives the same phase I within 0.2 % (), since phase I acts on the day-ahead schedule, which the trading mode does not change.
